Filipino

Since the 1980s, Filipino helpers have been a reliable option in Singapore. They are popular for their reliability, strong work ethic and good command of English, and excel in roles from caregiving to household chores. They also receive certified training before coming here.

Indonesian

For the last two decades, many Singaporeans have preferred hiring Indonesian helpers. They are known for being quick to adapt, emotionally intelligent and having many cultural similarities, which helps them get up to speed and settle in with most families.
