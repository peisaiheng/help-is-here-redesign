Questions

Eldercare, answered.

What if the helper is not suitable for my parent’s needs?

We assess suitability before we recommend anyone. If a placement would not suit your loved one’s needs, we will tell you honestly instead of pushing a candidate.

Can a helper care for someone with dementia, a stoma, a catheter or a feeding tube?

It depends on the person and on the helper. We learn what care is needed, assess whether a helper can realistically manage the role, and identify where additional professional training or support may be needed. We advise against placements that would not be suitable.

Do you help with AIC caregiving grants?

We help families navigate relevant AIC caregiving grants and practical care arrangements. Eligibility and amounts are set by AIC, so we will point you to the right information for your situation.

How quickly can a helper start?

Deployment can take as little as two weeks for suitable cases, subject to candidate availability, approvals and case requirements. Tell us your timeline in your first message so we can plan around it.

What training do eldercare helpers have?

Our helpers attend a professional training course in eldercare so they can look after your loved ones, and need minimal instruction once they are home. Where your loved one has specific needs, we also identify whether extra training or support is needed.

Can you place a Mandarin-speaking helper?

Yes. We have access to Mandarin-speaking helpers, many with eldercare experience in Taiwan. Tell us your parent’s language and we will look for helpers who can talk with them comfortably.
