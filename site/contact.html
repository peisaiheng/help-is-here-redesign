General enquiries

Have a different question?

Our support team is always here for you. For a quick answer, see the questions below. For anything else, send us a message and we will get back to you within 1 to 3 business days.

What happens after I send the form?

A Care Advisor will contact you within 1 business day to explain how we work and learn more about your situation. If we are not the right fit, we will say so.

Which nationalities can I choose from?

Our helpers come mainly from the Philippines, Indonesia and Myanmar. If you prefer another nationality, tell us and we will do our best to find a suitable helper.

What training do your helpers have?

Helpers are equipped with the necessary skills and relevant experience before work commences. They also attend an enhancement training course to standardise their home-care skills, including infant care and eldercare.
